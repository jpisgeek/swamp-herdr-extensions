## Swamp Club palette

`themes/swamp-club.json` takes its colors from the Swamp Club palette by Don
O'Neill: the 16 terminal colors, background, foreground, cursor and selection
from [sntxrr/ghostty-swamp-club](https://github.com/sntxrr/ghostty-swamp-club)
(commit `c275b3bfd6a39e0eabb7a826732f8997cc0ae6f2`), and the panel colors from
the `[theme.custom]` tokens of
[sntxrr/herdr-swamp-club](https://github.com/sntxrr/herdr-swamp-club)

`surface_dim`, `surface0`, `surface1`, `active_row_bg`, `selection_bg`,
